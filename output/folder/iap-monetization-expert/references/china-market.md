# China Market Notes

Use this file when the request touches mainland China distribution, billing, or monetization constraints.

## iOS

For digital unlocks inside iOS apps, assume Apple IAP remains the default mechanism unless a current jurisdiction-specific rule clearly changes that conclusion.

## Android

Mainland Android is often multi-channel rather than Google Play only.

Common realities:

- OEM app stores matter
- billing stacks differ by channel
- revenue share and tooling differ by distributor
- entitlement handling can become fragmented

## Product Strategy Implications

When advising on China:

- separate Google Play Android from mainland Android
- do not assume one subscription stack covers every channel
- plan entitlement reconciliation early
- verify channel billing policy before recommending a flow

## Risk Reminder

If a recommendation relies on web checkout, mini-program flows, or external payments, explicitly call out platform policy risk and the fact that the correct answer can depend on platform, app category, and current rules.
